      Data Quality & Transparency Declarations

      Topological Routing Accuracy

      Route calculations are 100% mathematically verified against the physical graph. Edge distances reconcile with reported segment totals within 0.05 km tolerance.

      Disaster Predictive Accuracy

      Real-world predictive accuracy for floods and landslides is Not established. The dataset contains hydrologic and terrain exposure covariates without ground-truth historical disaster event labels.

      Evidence-Based Confidence

      Confidence scores are computed strictly from measurable factors: endpoint snapping proximity (35%), graph component scale (30%), covariate coverage (20%), and mathematical validation (15%).
